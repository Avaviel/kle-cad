Design a keyboard, print the plates

  1. Design your keyboard in the KLE-CAD editor.
  2. Split the board into modules: Add Corner walks the outline of each half or block, and the dashed line is the plate silhouette.
  3. Take the layout to YACB: copy it here, paste it there, and download DXF drawings (plates, bosses, back-cut, hotswap) for Fusion 360.

Step-by-step video instructions are coming to YouTube (@avaviel).
